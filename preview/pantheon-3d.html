Sculpting the world…

The Look control filters the live 3D scene on the graphics card, so you can still rotate and zoom. Realistic supersamples the scene for smooth edges, adds contact shadows where surfaces meet, filmic tone, a soft vignette and fine grain. Retro draws it at low resolution like a late-90s pre-rendered game: crisp detail, contact shadows in the creases, a 16-bit colour depth with fine dithering and a thin outline around units. Painted adds a brush-stroke finish and fewer tones. All textures and models here are generated in code: grass, sand, snow, marble, bronze, chain mail, fur and cloth are painted from noise when the page loads, and the units are assembled from smooth sculpted parts. That keeps the game a single folder with nothing to download. For a fully realistic look, artist-made models (glTF files) could replace any of these one at a time without changing the game rules.
